01

LoanShield: five agents, one workflow

A multi-agent lending risk platform. Each agent has one job, and a person steps in when the case needs judgment.

1 · OrchestratorRoutes the application
2 · GatekeeperChecks it is complete
3 · FinancialScores the applicant
4 · FraudLooks for red flags
5 · ExplanationsWrites the decision letter
Human reviewScores of 40–69 and incomplete applications are sent to a person. Rejections get an ECOA-compliant adverse action letter.
